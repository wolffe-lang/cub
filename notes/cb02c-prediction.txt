MEASURED TODAY (30 Hz, seed 1, paw 1, the native build)
- Pace: the trot is 42 FP units a tick (256 a cell) = 4.92 cells/s on the straight (east along
  the shore, unblocked), 4.80 cells/s on the diagonal; a quarter turn (east to south) takes 4
  ticks at 4.83 cells/s of path, a turning radius of 0.41 cells. From a stand to the trot 11
  ticks; from the let-go (hold expired) to a stop 5 more ticks. Sneak 21 (2.46 cells/s).
  Foes: stalk 44 (5.16 cells/s), retreat 48, the same turn rate (16) and acceleration (4) as the cub.
- Wag: the tail is tied to the tick: walking it swings every 2 ticks, a period of 4 ticks
  (0.13 s, 7.5 Hz) over 2 frames; standing and sneaking it never moves (period 1, one frame).
  The walking paws flip on the same 2-tick swing.
- Paths: the shore and the thorn route draw gravel (rich 247 on 240, glyphs space · ․ ░; plain 7
  on 8, glyphs space . , :); the four coast branches and the coast's log branch draw grass (rich 64
  on 234, glyphs ⠖ ⠶; plain 2 on 0, glyphs ' and "): the forest's ground colour, not the path's.
- Body census over the 47 real-key scripts (60,787 script-ticks; scripts nest, so families share
  ticks): a live ground foe's drawn footprint shared a cell with the cub's on 1,879 ticks (1,935
  foe-ticks); of those, 1,043 foe-ticks were out of reach of a bite aimed straight at the foe
  (centre distance over 2 cells) and 493 lay outside the cone the cub faced. Footprints touching
  or overlapping: 3,692 foe-ticks, 2,604 of them out of an aimed bite's reach, 760 behind.
  Foe/foe footprint overlap: 71,580 pair-ticks (the cache guards spawn one cell apart).
  The cause: bodies collide as points 1 cell apart (`near(.., FP)`), the drawings since cb02b are
  4x4 cells (the cub north) to 8x2 (east), so foes stand inside the cub's drawing; and the bite
  reaches 2 cells from the centre, so a foe drawn touching the cub's flank or tail is often out of it.
- Fox chase (instrument: fox 5 cells behind a trotting cub, both at speed): on the straight the
  gap closes 5.00 -> 4.53 cells in 60 ticks (the fox keeps up and gains); through six 8-tick
  east/south legs it closes 5.00 -> 2.89 in 48 ticks: today the fox GAINS in the turns (pursuit
  cuts the corners at the same turn rate), it does not lose there.
