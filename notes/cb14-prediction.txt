What will be built, and the numbers I expect to read afterward.

1. The graph. Thirteen areas (seven today): the Stand (7) between Bramblehold
   and Larkmeadow, the Burn (8) between Larkmeadow and the Teeth, the Mere (9)
   between the Teeth and the Shallows, the Underteeth (10) as a branch the
   Teeth <-> the Hush, the Howe (11) between the Hush and Gravehollow, and
   Gravehollow (12) split off area 5: area 5 keeps its id and the cold den and
   becomes the Hush (the deep wood the bible's section 2 and 16 name; cb04 had
   folded the dark wood and the old den into one area), Gravehollow is the
   short last area holding the stone mouth and the alpha's arena. I expect
   this split to be the one placement the maintainer may change (R-cb14-3).
   Edges: 10 today; after, 10 kept (four of the path edges re-pointed) plus
   8 path edges and 5 shortcut edges = 23. Dens: four new (12 the root hollow,
   13 the kiln, 14 the dry island, 15 the bone cache) filling the save's
   reserved 12..15 exactly; the Howe and Gravehollow none (R-cb14-1 A).
   Lengths: the Stand, the Burn, the Mere, the Howe 224; the Underteeth 192;
   the Hush 160 (its arena rows cut); Gravehollow 128. Journey length (cells
   of spine along the way on, the Underteeth apart): before 160 + 240 + 288 +
   288 + 224 + 224 + 224 = 1648; after 1648 - 224 + 160 + 128 + 4 x 224 =
   2608, plus the Underteeth's 192 off the line: about 1.6x. Dens on the way
   on: 9 -> 12; bosses 4 -> 4 (none added: the heron and the sentries are
   cb18's foes, not bosses).

2. Save and replay. CUB 5 -> CUB 6: a slain mask per area (13), a `gates`
   word (the burnt oak, the stone mouth), shortcuts 0..255 (four new bits),
   caches widened past 16 bits for the alcoves. A CUB 5 file migrates with
   empty masks for the six new areas and gates 0; CUB 4 as before through
   5. The replay snapshot `journey 4` (37 values) is still read; this build
   writes `journey 5` with 44 values. Every committed case keeps replaying
   (their snapshots are journey 4 or none); the goldens of cases that cross
   or stand in the Teeth, the Shallows, area 5 and the boss arenas move (new
   branches, the renamed area 5, the arenas' radii); I predict about 40 of
   the 197 case directories move and 14 new ones appear (seven area first
   screens at two tiers), the opening, title, score, HUD, posture and Shingle
   cases unchanged.

   unreached, path_off 0, nearest foe >= 30; den spacing least within an
   area >= 110 and least onward >= 110 along the whole line (the Howe and
   Gravehollow carry no den, the Underteeth is measured apart); bends 0
   reversals on every route and branch including the new areas' (the branch
   shapes are cb05's); spurs every end named; foe census: the old areas'
   counts unchanged (9/16/18/18/8/8/9), the new areas 4..6 each (alcove
   guards only: the route tables are cb18's and empty here); gen-bench every

   Underteeth's per-cell rules are the costliest: I predict under 20 ms).
   The boss census: every world still won within five lives; the boar's
   first-attempt share may fall a little with the larger clearing (more
   room for the charge), the elk's shallows at 18 and Gravehollow at 20
   change nothing in attempts; arena_breaches 0.
