cb05 — the wide forest: prediction, written before the first change
======================================================================

Base: cub trunk 7e2c6a77 (+ cb04d's note, 5b10fec). Written 2026-10-26 before any
implementation commit; the "before" rows are read from trunk's code and notes, and
the instruments added by this lane re-measure them on trunk's own generators before
the generators move (notes/cb05-evidence/*-before.txt).

Dens: count and spacing
-----------------------
Before (trunk): 11 dens in 6 areas: Shingle 1 (0); Bramblehold 3 (1, 2, 8);
Larkmeadow 4 (3, 9, 10, 11); the Teeth 1 (4); the Shallows 1 (5); Gravehollow 1 (6).
cb03a's feel audit measured pairwise Manhattan spacing 45-114 in Bramblehold and
22-149 in Larkmeadow (160-cell areas); route (tile-walk) spacing not measured before:
the new `--den-spacing` census measures it on trunk's generators first.

Prediction after (R-cb05-3, recommended A: at most two dens an area, at least 110
route cells between dens): 9 dens in 7 areas: Shingle 1 (0); Cinderwick 1 (7, the
cold hearth); Bramblehold 2 (2 the briar hollow about a third in, 1 the split roots
about two thirds); Larkmeadow 2 (3 the grass nest at the entry, 11 the last warm
hollow past the middle); the Teeth, the Shallows, Gravehollow 1 each at their entries.
Dens 8 (the coal roots), 9 (the low embers) and 10 (the ash shelter) retired to
landmarks: `den_exists` false, their ids kept in the save format, a save whose last
den is one of them wakes at the nearest kept den (a migration test). Predicted route
spacing: no pair under 110 within an area or across an edge over 50 seeds and both
fields; the minimum about 110-140 (Bramblehold's two), the maximum about 300
(the Shingle's den to the cold hearth through the thorn gate).

The bend census
---------------
cb04d measured reversals of the centreline's transverse step within three samples
per ten sampled route cells: Shingle 0.0895, Bramblehold 0.1857, Larkmeadow 0.1325,
Teeth 0.1681, Shallows 0.1903, Gravehollow 0.1699 (route medians 7/9/9/7/9/9 wide).
The new census (`--bends-audit`): along every route and branch, sampled a cell at a
time, the turn sign between the heading four cells back and four ahead; a reversal is
two counted turns of opposite sign within eight cells. Prediction before: the cache
branches (`forest.branch`, `terrain.meander`'s alternating 16-cell anchors) show
reversals within eight cells on most of the 400 inland branch rows (50 seeds x 2
fields x 2 areas x 2 caches); after: 0 reversals on every route and branch, with the
branches drawn from four shapes (a slow S, a hook, a loop, a 180-degree turn where
the field has room), never two alike in sight, and cb04d's per-ten measure no worse.

Generation and the frame
------------------------
Generation (the terrain layer classified once over an area's whole width and length
at field 200: about 217 x 289 cells) is not measured before; predicted under 50 ms an
area after, on hasu's container (x86-64, release), with the per-cell path test
early-rejecting by bounding boxes. Render means (cb04d, Mac arm64: 182/174 us at
80x24 rich/plain, 1306/1296 at 200x60; Linux 297/278 and 2142/2138) predicted within
+15 % after and inside the gate's bars (1000 / 4000 us at slack 1); latency medians
unchanged (Mac about 23 ms, Linux about 14 ms).
