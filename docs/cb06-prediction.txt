cb06 prediction, before implementation

R-cb06-1 recommendation: 900 ticks / 30 seconds at 30 Hz. Enough time to read the crossing and recognise the fall without delaying a repeat journey.
R-cb06-2 recommendation: A, one decoded key skips the whole opening and performs no move; preserve the existing event and seen flag.

crack: 150 ticks / 5 seconds, one original part at 144 BPM in 4/4, three bars. Strike at 210, thinning hold, quickening figure at 300, end at the drop at 360. Title replaces at start, crack replaces it without a fade at 210, silence under the fall, Shingle at the first blink. Skipping immediately cancels crack (unlike gameplay death's uninterruptible sting).

Prediction: busiest 200x60 frame, actual Shingle under a blink mask, below 12 ms rich and 10 ms plain in the release build on kasumi CPUs 0-3. Measure rendered frame cost, not build time. Opening terrain is bounded to one grid plus linear overlays; no engine changes.

Tests: shot-boundary and skip tests, disjoint sky/ice/deep-water style sets, first blink never fully open, cue at first crack, all shot goldens on 80x24 / 120x40 / 200x60 and both tiers, unskipped and per-shot skip pty rows, PCM golden on Linux/macOS. Plants on cb06-plant-blink and cb06-plant-cue, draft PRs. Preserve replay syntax and save schema; old opening recordings intentionally adopt the new animation/timing, with compatibility documented.
